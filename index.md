Understand the opportunities and risks of using AI tools, including large language models, in research conducted on Sagehen HPC. This workshop covers the current AI landscape, Pomona College policies for AI use, data classification considerations, and guidance on whether to use local models on Sagehen or cloud-based AI services. You'll learn responsible practices for protecting sensitive research data, ensuring research integrity, and using AI tools ethically. This workshop is part of the Pomona College HPC Workshop Series.

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: callout

## Pomona College HPC Context

This workshop is part of the **Pomona College Research Computing Workshop Series**
for researchers using the **Sagehen HPC cluster**.

**Prerequisites:**

- Completed Workshop 13 (HPC Security Orientation)
- Understanding of data classification (Workshop 14) recommended
- Required for all users running AI/ML workloads on Sagehen
- A Sagehen HPC account (request from [its-hpc@pomona.edu](mailto:its-hpc@pomona.edu))
- Access to the [OnDemand portal](https://ondemand.hpc.pomona.edu)

For questions or support, contact the Research Computing team at
[its-hpc@pomona.edu](mailto:its-hpc@pomona.edu).

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::

Designed for all researchers and students, this workshop assumes no prior AI knowledge. You will gain practical understanding of how to responsibly leverage AI tools while protecting your research, respecting intellectual property, and adhering to Pomona College policies.
