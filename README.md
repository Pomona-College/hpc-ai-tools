# Responsible Use of AI Tools on HPC

Pomona College HPC Workshop Series

## Overview

This workshop addresses the growing use of AI tools, large language models, and generative AI in research and education. Participants learn Pomona College's AI policy, data classification and sensitivity considerations, when to use local HPC systems versus cloud AI services, how to run AI models on Sagehen HPC, and responsible use practices. The workshop covers compliance implications, data privacy, and ethical considerations for academic and research applications of AI.

## Episodes

1. **The AI Tools Landscape**: Survey modern AI tools and models, understand computational requirements, compare cloud-based versus local execution, identify responsible use considerations, and learn policy implications.
2. **Pomona College AI Policy**: Review institutional AI policies and guidelines, understand what tools are approved for use, identify restricted data that cannot be used with external AI services, and recognize compliance requirements.
3. **Data Classification for AI**: Classify data sensitivity levels, identify FERPA-protected information, recognize export-controlled research, understand medical data privacy rules, and determine which AI tools are appropriate for different data types.
4. **Local vs Cloud AI Execution**: Compare Sagehen HPC to cloud services like ChatGPT and Claude, understand data privacy implications, evaluate computational costs, and choose appropriate execution models for different use cases.
5. **Running Models Locally on Sagehen**: Set up open-source language models and AI tools on Sagehen, use GPU resources for inference, deploy models for research workflows, and optimize performance for local execution.
6. **Responsible AI Practices**: Follow ethical guidelines for AI use, document AI-assisted work, avoid inappropriate model applications, understand AI limitations and biases, and practice transparency in research using AI tools.

## Prerequisites

- Active Sagehen HPC cluster account (helpful but not required)
- Basic understanding of AI and machine learning concepts
- Familiarity with Pomona College data policies
- Interest in responsible use of AI tools in research
- No extensive technical background required

## Learning Objectives

After completing this workshop, learners will be able to:
- Understand Pomona College's AI policy and approval requirements
- Classify data types and determine appropriate AI tools for different data
- Recognize when restricted data cannot be sent to external AI services
- Choose between cloud and local AI execution based on data sensitivity
- Run open-source AI models and language models on Sagehen
- Practice responsible and ethical use of AI in research
- Document and disclose AI-assisted work in research outputs

## Target Audience

Researchers, students, and faculty at all levels who use or are considering using AI tools in their work. Particularly important for those working with sensitive data (FERPA, medical records, export-controlled research) or those seeking guidance on responsible AI practices in academic research.

## Duration

Approximately 2-3 hours, mixing policy overview, technical demonstrations, and discussion of responsible use considerations.

## Technical Requirements

- Sagehen HPC cluster account (recommended for hands-on demos)
- SSH access for running local models
- CUDA toolkit for GPU-accelerated inference
- Optional: Docker or Singularity for containerized AI models
- Text editor for configuration files
- Optional: Ollama or similar tools for running local language models
